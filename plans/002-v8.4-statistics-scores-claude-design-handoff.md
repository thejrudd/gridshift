## Season Scoreboard

Design a season-level scoreboard showing every NFL week.

### Desktop

- Present a horizontally scrollable board with one column per week.
- Initially center the board on the current week.
- Show approximately three to five week columns when space allows.
- Keep the horizontal scrollbar or equivalent affordance discoverable without dominating the interface.
- Include Weeks 1–18, followed by Wild Card, Divisional, Conference Championship, and Super Bowl.
- Group games chronologically by day and kickoff window within each week.

### Mobile and Tablet

- Show one selected week at a time.
- Use GridShift’s established horizontally scrollable week-selector pattern.
- Keep the selected week clearly visible and centered when practical.
- Avoid shrinking scorebugs until their labels become difficult to read.

### Controls

- Add a season selector with the current season selected by default.
- Include representative historical seasons in the mock scaffold.
- Include a quick way to return to the current week.

## Scorebug Design

Each matchup should use a compact, neutral broadcast scorebug.

### Core Team Presentation

- Two team rows.
- Team logo.
- Team abbreviation or name.
- Tabular score numerals.
- Clear home/away relationship without excessive labeling.

### Scheduled State

Show available pregame information such as:

- Kickoff day and time.
- Broadcast network.
- Venue or location.

### Live State

Show information when available:

- Quarter.
- Game clock.
- Possession.
- Down and distance.
- Field position.
- Red-zone state.
- Remaining timeouts.

Do not reserve awkward empty space when a provider does not supply one of these fields.

### Additional States

Provide designed examples for:

- Halftime.
- Delayed.
- Postponed.
- Final.
- Final in overtime.
- Partial data.
- Unavailable data.
- Offline cached data.
- Error state.

### Final Games

- Emphasize the winner through typography and hierarchy.
- Slightly mute the losing team.
- Do not communicate the winner through color alone.

### Favorite-Team Matchup

- Keep the matchup in its correct chronological position.
- Add a prominent team-gradient edge or header using the existing team theme utilities.
- Add a clear Favorite badge.
- Give the card stronger hierarchy without making it visually incompatible with surrounding games.
- Do not apply team gradients to every scorebug.

### Motion

Use restrained broadcast-like feedback:

- Brief score-change flash.
- Small LIVE pulse.
- Smooth possession-indicator transition.

All motion must degrade cleanly or disappear under reduced-motion preferences.

## Game Drilldown

Selecting a matchup should open a detailed game view. Use normalized mock data and design these four sections.

### 1. Overview

- Broadcast score hero.
- Game status and venue context.
- Quarter-by-quarter line score.
- Key player leaders.
- Primary team-comparison statistics.
- Clear final winner treatment or live-game state.

### 2. Team Stats

Group statistics into:

- Offense.
- Defense.
- Situational.
- Discipline.
- Possession.

Use paired-value advantage rails where useful. A clearly favorable statistic may emphasize the stronger team. Contextual statistics—especially time of possession—should communicate proportion without declaring an objective winner.

Include explicitly labeled defensive views for:

- Passing yards allowed, derived from opponent passing production.
- Rushing yards allowed, derived from opponent rushing production.

Other representative statistics should include first downs, total yards, turnovers, sacks, third- and fourth-down efficiency, red-zone efficiency, penalties, and possession time.

### 3. Players

Provide complete, readable table designs for:

- Passing.
- Rushing.
- Receiving.
- Defense.
- Kicking.
- Punting.
- Returns.

Tables should remain usable at every density and viewport. Prefer responsive table treatment or deliberate column prioritization over illegible compressed text.

### 4. Scoring

Group scoring plays by quarter. Each entry should support:

- Time remaining.
- Scorer.
- Play description.
- Conversion or result.
- Resulting score.

The timeline should be easy to scan without becoming decorative or oversized.
