# Fantasy Live

Back: [[Home]]

Implementation reference for the **live layer in Fantasy Matchups**: the replay bar, the win chance / lead chart, the scoring plays list, and the desktop player hover cards. The separate Fantasy Live tab (`companionView: 'live'`) was retired; `/fantasy/live` now normalizes to `/fantasy/matchups` (`FANTASY_VIEW_ALIASES` in `src/utils/appRoutes.js`). File routing lives in [[Where To Edit]]; server/provider architecture lives in [[Live Data Server Architecture]].

## Where it appears

For the **current NFL week** (Sleeper `/state/nfl` via `resolveFantasyLiveAvailability()`) and **every earlier week of that season**, only for Sleeper leagues with a live session (allowlisted league, server-side BALLDONTLIE credential), and only once the week's games are known. Future weeks, other seasons (including the offseason), other platforms, and leagues without live access render Matchups exactly as before. Nothing in the masthead, rows, or drill-ins changes behavior when the layer is off.

**Finished weeks.** An earlier week (`historical` from the hook) does nothing beyond the session check until the league has a live session, so it never refreshes the shared Sleeper stat lines Matchups already shows. With a session it loads its games, stats, Sleeper rows and real plays once (never the server's dev mock plays, which are only a few big plays per player), with no polling (no live refresh, no Sleeper stat-line interval). It opens at the final whistle: the replay bar reads Final (no live dot or live mark, "Back to final"), and the chart has no live line. The chart is rebuilt from that week's plays alone; stored win-chance history is neither read nor written, so the week looks the same whether or not this browser watched it live. The free tier fetches no plays, as for the current week.

Every existing Matchups drill-in is preserved: player rows open `PlayerMatchupBreakdown`, position chips open Tale of the Tape (`PlayerMatchupCompare`), team scorecards open the team scoring drilldown (`TeamScoringDrilldown`, see Team scoring drilldown below), VS opens `MatchupPreviewModal`, and the week and matchup pickers are unchanged.

## Axis: kickoff windows

The chart, replay bar, and plays share one 0..1 axis built by `buildSlateAxis(games)`. Games whose kickoffs fall within 45 minutes of a window's first kickoff share that window (a 4:25 game joins the 4:05 window). Windows come from the week's actual kickoff times, so Thanksgiving (three Thursday windows plus Friday), Christmas, and Saturday slates need no special case. Each window's width is `max(1.5, games in window)`, so a single Thursday or Monday game gets less room than a Sunday slate but stays usable. Labels use Eastern time: the day alone when a day has one window ("Thu", "Mon"), otherwise day and time ("Sun 1p", "Sun 4:05p").

A play sits at its window's start plus its own game progress (`getPlayProgress()`) through the window. NOW (`nowProgress`) is the furthest point any game has reached. The dev sandbox's `getSlateProgressForGameProgress()` and `getReplayChartProgress()` use the same axis.

## Replay

The page opens at live (`replayAt = null`), or at the final whistle for a finished week. The bar scrubs (drag, arrow keys step between plays, Home/End), steps to the previous or next scoring play, and plays forward to live; it never moves past live. While replaying:

- the masthead shows "Score at this moment", the win chance and projected finals at that point (a settled matchup too: it returns to its official final, projection gap and bench points only at the end);
- starter rows show each player's points and started state at that point, and "Yet to finish" counts follow it;
- the scoring plays list keeps every play up to live, dims the ones after that point (`.is-after`, still clickable to jump forward), and counts "N of M plays so far", so a selected play is never a ceiling on the list;
- hover cards describe the game as it stood then.

Clicking a scoring play (in the list, a dot on the chart, or a play in a hover card's list) rewinds the whole page to just after it and marks it on the chart with a guide, ring, and callout. Selecting it again clears the mark. If a play filter hides the selected play, the filter resets to Both. A selection from the chart (or a hover card) scrolls the open Scoring plays list to centre that play's row; a click in the list itself never moves the page. Replay position, selection, filters, and the hover card reset at the matchup-navigation boundary (matchup or week change).

At live, rows and the masthead read the hook's displayed totals — Sleeper's points plus still-pending plays — so the chart, plays, rows, and masthead agree on one number. Once the matchup is settled, the masthead returns to its official final.

## Chart

Win chance (default) or Lead, from the viewer's side. Win chance is a line on 0–100% with 50% marked; Lead is a step line around "Tied". Both shade above the midline in the viewer's fantasy color and below it in the opponent's. The Lead y-axis fits the largest margin seen so far (and the projected final margin), never less than ±10, and grows as the margin does. A dashed line runs from now to the projected final margin. Every play with fantasy points moves the line, so scrubbing shows the score change gradually. Only notable plays get a dot (`isNotablePlay()` in `matchupReplay.js`): any score (touchdown, field goal, extra point, safety, two-point conversion), any turnover (thrown, lost, or forced by a fantasy defense or IDP), or any single play worth 6+ points either way, judged on the play's own points (`rawPts`) rather than the reconciled display total. Hovering or dragging within 20px of a dot snaps the crosshair, tooltip, and replay onto that play (with a ring and a pointer cursor); clicking there selects it. Tapping or dragging elsewhere scrubs. The future beyond now is dimmed; a red line marks live. Before the first scoring play the hook supplies a two-point forecast line so the chart still renders.

## Scoring plays

Only plays from this week's games that have kicked off (server mock plays included). Uniform 60px flat rows, newest first, with a 3px edge in the scoring side's color, the play text, window and clock, the player's running total, fantasy points, and the change in the viewer's win chance. Filters: Both / Mine / Theirs / Big plays (touchdowns, negatives, and swings at `getBigPlayThreshold()`). Pending and stat-update rows keep their labels.

## Player hover cards

Mouse and keyboard only: opened by a pointer event whose `pointerType` is `mouse` or by `:focus-visible` keyboard focus, never by touch, pen, or a tap's focus. It is not gated on a `(hover: hover)` media query, because some desktop browsers (Edge on touch-capable machines) report no hover while a mouse is in use. 120ms to open, instant between rows, hidden on click (a pending open is cancelled too). Opened by the mouse, the card follows the pointer (below-right of it, flipping left or above near the frame's right edge or the window's bottom); it positions itself from window `pointermove` straight on the DOM, at most once per animation frame, so following never re-renders the page. The side it sits on is chosen when it opens and kept while it still fits, so small moves near an edge never flip it back and forth, and the opening pointer position is adopted once per row, so a re-render (a live tick) never snaps it back. Matchups player rows carry no native `title` tooltip; the card replaces it. Opened by keyboard focus, it sits below or above the row. Before kickoff: points per game, points by week against this week's projection, season totals, and best week (from the starter's `weekly` rows scored with `calcPoints`). While the game is live: points against the pace target (projection × game progress), this game's stat line summed from its plays' stat deltas, and the last three plays. Once final: points against projection, the stat line, and the best play. Without the live layer the game views use the week's stat row. Stat chips follow the player's position (`buildStatChips` in `MatchupPlayerHoverCard.jsx`): QB passing (plus INTs and rushing when present), RB/WR/TE rushing and receiving with TDs, K field goals and extra points, IDP players (DL/LB/DB and their aliases) tackles, sacks (always for linemen), TFL, INTs, passes defended (`idp_pd` or Sleeper's `idp_pass_def`) and forced fumbles, up to four chips, and team DEF sacks, takeaways, TDs and points allowed (a game, over a season). A defender never shows receiving chips. A click always opens the existing player breakdown.

## Team scoring drilldown

`src/components/companion/TeamScoringDrilldown.jsx` (+ `.css`, `tsd-*` classes) opens from either team scorecard and replaced the old By category / By player sheet. Game state picks the layout; there are no phase tabs: **Projection board** before any of that side's starters kick off, **Scoreboard** while any has (until the matchup is settled), **Beat or missed** once `matchupIsSettled`.

- Lineup spots come from `enrichedSlots` in league roster order (`league.roster_positions` without BN/IR), so DEF, SUPER_FLEX and IDP spots appear and an unfilled spot reads Empty. Labels use `SLOT_LABELS`.
- The drilldown follows the live layer's current points (`liveDisplayedPoints`) and game states (`starterGameStateById`), never a replay moment, so its categories match the stat lines they explain.
- `src/utils/teamScoringDrilldownModel.js` adds no projection or scoring source: projected points per starter are `resolveStarterProjection()` (the values the masthead's `expectedA/B` sums), projected categories are `buildPlayerProjectionBreakdown()` (once settled, the recorded pregame baseline when one exists), actual categories are `buildFantasyMatchupScoringBreakdown()` reconciled to each starter's displayed points, and the live projected finish is the `PACE_PROJECTION_MODEL` outlook `paceExpectedA/B` sums. Gaps stay visible as rows in the **Other** group — the shared `model` group, relabelled for this drilldown: "Projection adjustment" (a projection's gap to its own stat line), "Season average estimate" / "Position estimate" (no stat line), "Other Scoring Adjustments" (provider points no stat explains) and "League scoring adjustment" (to the reported team total).
- Scoring groups (`TEAM_SCORING_GROUPS`) run offence first, then defence: Passing, Rushing, Receiving, Bonuses, Kicking, Special teams, D/ST, IDP, Negative plays, Other. Bars and legends keep that order rather than sorting by size. A team defence's or IDP player's points always land in D/ST or IDP (`getTeamScoringGroupId()` checks the player's position before the stat key, so an IDP pass defended is never Passing). Each group has its own `--color-scoring-*` token (src/index.css, [[Design Tokens]]); no two groups share a colour. Deductions (Negative plays) are hatched over the end of each bar so the clean part is the net.
- Pregame: "Where the projection comes from" and By position cross-filter each other and the category table — a group dims the other segments and switches each spot's value to that group; a spot fills each group segment to its share and switches the legend to its values. Live: By position fills each spot's points so far by group against a projection tick; Biggest so far follows a selected spot. Final: the difference from projection sits under the final score; "Where the points came from" splits the final by group and cross-filters with By position (each spot's full final points by group against its projection, with the difference) and a table of every category that scored (stat and points only — no projection columns, so a missed projection never reads as negative points). Pregame's category table sums each category across the lineup (one row per stat, no player column; with a spot selected it is that player's rows). Live and Final category rows name their player unless a lineup spot is selected.
- Selection only changes classes and widths; bars keep their elements, so nothing redraws. Bars grow once on mount.
- ESPN leagues derive missing stat lines with `loadEspnFantasyGameLogWeekRow()` (moved from the old sheet).

## Layout

**Before kickoff.** Until any starter in the matchup has kicked off (the hook's `started`, read from the week's game feed alone, as early as Thursday night), the page is plain Matchups: the masthead keeps its Estimated win chance bar, and there is no replay bar, chart, or plays list. Hovering a team scorecard with a mouse (or focusing it from the keyboard) shows `MatchupPointSourcesCard`. Like the player hover cards, it opens on a `mouse` `pointerType` or `:focus-visible`, never touch, pen or a tap's focus, is not gated on a `(hover: hover)` media query, and hides on click. It splits whatever the header shows for that side by position, so its rows always add up to the header number; any gap shows as League adjustments, and clicking still opens the team scoring drilldown. Pregame (both header scores are projections): "Where the points come from", projected points by position with each starter's share, built by `buildMatchupPointSources()` (`src/utils/matchupPointSources.js`) from the side's win-probability outlooks; starters without a projection are marked as estimates. Live (live masthead, a replay moment, or both sides scoring): "Points so far", built by `buildMatchupScoredSources()` from the same points and game states as the rows (`displaySlots`, following the replay moment), with a playing / yet to play / finished count, playing starters dotted, starters yet to play muted, and the header's projected final with the points still to go. Final (settled, not replaying): "Where the points came from", the official points by position, each position's +/− against the projection the header's delta uses (recorded pregame baseline, else the current external projection, shown only when every starter in the position has one), and the top bench scorer.

**Arrival.** The page holds `MatchupSkeleton` until the live hook's `ready` (its first answer: no layer applies, no session, an error, or this context's games, plays and stat line loaded; latched per matchup so a later refetch never returns the page to the skeleton), capped at 2.5s per matchup (`LIVE_LAYER_MAX_WAIT_MS`), so the page never paints plain Matchups and then jumps to the live layout. The live pieces then enter on the shared loading tokens while `.is-revealing` is on the page frame (`LIVE_REVEAL_WINDOW_MS`, 800ms): the replay bar, the chart's label row and the tabs fade (they are sticky, so no transform), and the scoring plays stagger in as a `gridshift-reveal--auto` list. The window opens on page load, a week or matchup change, and a return to the Matchups tab; a Lineup / Scoring plays switch never replays it, and play rows that mount later (scrubbing, new plays) appear without motion. The chart itself waits for the hook's `feedReady` (every started game's plays in, or none expected, and Sleeper's stat line landed), capped separately at 10s (`LIVE_CHART_MAX_WAIT_MS`); until then a chart-sized placeholder stands in. It then mounts once per matchup (keyed by league/season/week/matchup) and its SVG wipes in left to right on mount, so it arrives whole instead of redrawing as each game's plays land. Once shown it stays for that matchup (a game kicking off later never swaps it back to the placeholder). See [[Loading Motion]].

**After kickoff.** Order at every width: Matchups controls, replay bar, masthead (without the Estimated win chance bar; Forecast details stay), the full-width Win chance / Lead chart, then **Lineup** and **Scoring plays** tabs: the shared `.season-tab` underline tabs, left aligned like every other GridShift tab row. The chart sits above the tabs so it is in view on both. Lineup is the default tab. Both panels stay mounted and in layout; the inactive one is collapsed, invisible, and `inert` (`.is-inactive`) rather than `display: none`, which would restart its row reveal on every switch. Selecting a play does not switch tabs. The replay bar is a direct child of the page frame and sticks to the scroll container's edge (`top` cancels `.content-area`'s `pt-4`/`lg:pt-6`); the tabs stick under it via `--matchup-replay-h`, and each panel's label row sticks under the tabs (`--matchup-live-tabs-h`). The plays list is one continuous list. Section descriptions live in the Guide, not on the page.

The bench always shows (the Bench toggle was removed). Starter rows drop the weather line once that player's game is final.

**Replay bar sizing.** "Back to live" / "Back to final" is a filled signature button so it reads as the way back while replaying. The status column is a fixed `12.5rem`, because its text changes as the replay moves and an auto column would resize the track under the pointer mid-drag. The track's grab area is taller than it draws (36px, 44px on touch). A drag keeps its own thumb position and seeks the page as a React transition, so a slow page render never holds the thumb back; the chart's drag does the same.

## Data rules carried over

- **Reconciliation.** BDL plays are the only feed source in connected live and replay; Sleeper reconciles them and never writes a row of its own. A play stays pending until Sleeper's numbers account for it, then becomes confirmed. A play's own `pts` is never rewritten; the residual between Sleeper's total and confirmed plays is pinned to the latest confirmed play as `adjustment`, and `displayPts = pts + adjustment` is what every surface consumes. Displayed player total = displayed provider rows + stat-update rows + residual = Sleeper's total plus still-pending play points. Unconfirmed provider plays stay in reconciliation state but are omitted from the feed and chart. A single `stat-update` row covers stats no play explains after a grace period (two stat-line fetches 30s apart). A starter is settled (`settledPlayerIds`) once their game is final in the week's game feed and that game's plays have arrived (or no plays will come, as on the free tier); settling before the plays arrive would hand the reconciler a stand-in for points the plays then also count. A settled starter skips that grace — a finished game is fetched once, so the second fetch never comes — and, if Sleeper credits points that no confirmed play or row explains (no stat line, every play unmatched or unconfirmed), gets a points-only row for the residual (rule 13). A play that later arrives for that starter retires the points-only row and its carried residual, so the play confirms instead of counting on top. Either way every Sleeper point reaches a feed row, so the replay and the chart reach the official total at the end of each player's game rather than jumping at the matchup's final close. A finished game's stat-update rows take that game's time at their axis spot (kickoff + progress × 3h15m) rather than their creation time, so the win chance at earlier moments includes them in order. Stat-update rows move the line but never get a dot. For a settled starter the stat line also never goes stale (a finished week fetches it once, so the 90s freshness window would otherwise let plays Sleeper never credited count again), nothing is left pending (rule 14: plays surviving the stat-line and points checks are confirmed, since pending points count on top of Sleeper's final), and a negative residual — estimated plays, such as IDP tackles priced as solo, running past Sleeper — is spread across their positive confirmed plays in proportion to each play's points (rule 15) rather than landing on the last one. Each settled starter's rows therefore total Sleeper exactly, every play stays visible and positive, and the running total tracks the official one through the game.
- **IDP tackles.** A tackle play keeps a generic `idp_tkl` stat but is priced the league's way when the league pays solo / assisted tackles instead (one tackler named reads as solo, several as assisted), so IDP tackles appear as plays rather than only in a stat-update row. Reconciliation counts tackles as one family; see [[Scoring Call Sites]].
- **Polling.** Sleeper matchup points refresh on every live tick (5s paid, 60s free) with BDL games and stats; Sleeper weekly stat lines poll every 30s only while a matchup starter's game is live. Stat lines confirm plays for 90s after a successful fetch, then confirmation falls back to points. The hook keys its starter list and team set by content so a refresh that returns the same starters does not re-create the fetch callback (this previously caused a continuous refetch loop).
- **Win probability.** `src/data/liveWinProbabilityModel.js` is the only coefficient contract. Starter projections change only in `resolveStarterProjection()`. Each historical point is reconstructed per starter from points observed by that moment and that starter's game progress; the live close uses the current snapshot. Exact 0%/100% requires every starter final (or a confirmed bye) and a fresh no-store Sleeper response with both final team totals and every starter's official points.
- **Play-by-play caching.** The server archives finished games' plays (see [[Live Data Server Architecture]]), and the hook keeps finished games' plays for the tab's visit (`FINAL_GAME_PLAYS`, bounded at 400 games; never for the dev sandbox or mock plays), so returning to a week or switching matchups asks nothing again. The hook keys the week's games, plays, Sleeper rows and stat lines by the week (and matchup) they belong to and reads other weeks' values as empty, so right after a week switch no other week's data reaches the feed, the chart, or the reconciler.
- **Week.** The current week comes from Sleeper `/state/nfl`; the Matchups week picker chooses it or an earlier week of the same season (`useMatchupLiveFeed({ week })`). Preseason, offseason, and leagues from other seasons stay weekless. Do not add a week picker to the live layer — weeks belong to the normal Matchups week picker.

## Dev sandbox

With `VITE_LIVE_SANDBOX=true`, Matchups renders the sandbox's synthetic two-roster league (2025 Week 12) through `FantasyBaseOverride` (`src/context/SleeperContext.jsx`) when the route names no week or that week, with the sandbox panel mounted. Any other week falls through to the connected league, whose live layer reads the real routes (`allowSandbox: false` makes the hook skip the sandbox base and `liveDataSource.js`). The sandbox base supplies season totals derived from its weekly stat lines, and starts the allowlisted live session itself before its first data request (`ensureSandboxSession()`), because Matchups can ask for matchups before the live layer has started one. `?liveSandbox=off` opts out. Production builds alias the sandbox to `production-stub.js`.
