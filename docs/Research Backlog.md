# Research Backlog

These are technology and data-source evaluations, not committed product features. Do not treat them as release promises. Promote an idea to TO_DO.md only after its user problem, product fit, costs, licensing, and data boundaries are clear.

## Writing and editing tools

- **Open Pencil** — Investigate whether its drafting, editing, and text-workflow concepts could inform future player narratives, matchup writeups, trade explanations, export copy, or guided content-generation tools.
- **Pretext** — Investigate whether rich-text or structured-editor concepts could support in-app notes, report building, annotations, or editorial workflows tied to Trade, Draft Coach, or Statistics drilldowns.

## Data sources

- **LeagueLogs fantasy API** — Evaluate whether it can supplement Trade, Companion, or Scout with redraft/dynasty player values, rookie-pick values, Sleeper-keyed player data, player status/news blurbs, or NFL state. Verify provenance, refresh cadence, attribution, durability, and licensing before use.
