# Privacy Policy

*Last updated: July 22, 2026*

GridShift is designed to work primarily in your browser. This policy explains what information the app stores, where it is sent, and the choices available to you.

## Information stored in your browser

GridShift stores your season predictions, theme and display preferences, selected team, onboarding and tour status, and connected league details in your browser's local storage. It also keeps short-lived caches of sports and player data to make the app faster. This data stays on your device unless you export it, connect a service, or use a hosted GridShift server.

You can remove this data through the app's reset/disconnect controls or by clearing this site's browser storage. Exported prediction files are created only when you choose to export them.

## Connected fantasy services

When you connect a Sleeper league, GridShift sends the username and league identifiers you provide to the Sleeper API and receives league, roster, matchup, and player information. GridShift stores the selected-league state locally in your browser.

Optional GridShift Live access stores an encrypted, short-lived cookie containing the allowed league reference and provider. The server uses it to control access to live data.

## Data providers and network requests

The app requests relevant sports, fantasy, weather, and market data from the providers listed in the in-app Attributions screen. Those providers process requests under their own privacy policies. A self-hosted GridShift installation may use different providers or configuration.

## No advertising analytics

The open-source app does not include advertising trackers or third-party analytics. Operators of a hosted or self-hosted deployment may have their own server, hosting, or access logs; their practices are outside this repository's control.

## Changes and contact

If this policy changes materially, the updated date above will change. For questions or privacy requests, open an issue in the [GridShift repository](https://github.com/thejrudd/nfl-predictor/issues).
